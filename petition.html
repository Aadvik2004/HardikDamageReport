WHY WE FIGHT

The Beginning 💙

I have been a Mumbai Indians fan since 2012. I watched this team when they had nothing — no titles, no legacy, just raw potential and a fanbase that believed anyway. I watched Ricky Ponting make one of the greatest decisions in IPL history — stepping down from captaincy and handing it to a young Rohit Sharma. Nobody knew then what that moment would become.

The Rise 👑

What followed was nothing short of a dynasty. 5 titles in 8 years. Rohit took a team of chokers and turned them into champions. The 2013 and 2015 titles — back to back — built the foundation. Then came the sheer dominance of 2017 and 2019. And 2020, where we won it again proving it was no fluke.

I remember 2022 — we lost our first 8 games. Finished last. I still wore the blue. I still believed. Because that's what being an MI fan means. You don't leave when it gets hard.

Even in 2023, with a weakened squad, we clawed our way back and reached Qualifier 2. Under pressure, this team always found a way. Because of the culture Rohit built. Because of what this franchise stood for.

The Fall 💀

Then came Hardik Pandya.

Since he took over in 2024, Mumbai Indians have been an embarrassment. Fans booed him at Wankhede — their own home ground. Not because they are cruel. But because they are broken. Because they watched something they built over 15 years get dismantled in two seasons.

In 2024 we finished last. In 2025 we scraped to Qualifier 2 with decisions that baffled fans and analysts alike. And in 2026 we are staring at yet another wooden spoon finish.

Hardik Pandya as a captain has scored runs at just 134 SR with 20 AVG and has picked just 4 wickets at 11.45 eco. And on the field, he looks absolutely deflated when a partnership starts forming for the opposition. He doesn't even have guts to take any bold decisions. Such spineless decision-making have no place in our coveted franchise.

Never in 15 years of supporting this team have I felt ashamed of their performances. Not in 2022. Not ever. Until now.

The Suspicion 🕵️

Hardik Pandya has performed disastrously since 2024 — especially against CSK, with both bat and ball. His decision making is questionable at best and sabotage at worst. He has destroyed our head to head record against every team. He has revealed our strategies. He has prevented us from winning our 6th title before CSK gets theirs.

I am not saying it lightly — but the evidence points to one conclusion. Hardik Pandya is a CSK Dhurandhar. Sent to destroy us from within.

What We Want 🏆

I have supported MI through their build, their dominance, their downfall, and their transition. I will keep supporting them. I bleed blue through thick and thin, always. But this team deserves better leadership. We as fans deserve better from the franchise. Rohit Sharma doesn't deserve to see his empire, his efforts, the blood and sweat he has put to make the franchise what it is today being blown to dust. Wankhede deserves to be a fortress again.

If you are an MI fan who has felt this pain — sign the petition. Let the franchise know that we see what is happening. And we will not stay silent.
